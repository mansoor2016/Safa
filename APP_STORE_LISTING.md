### Screenshot Capture Tips

- **Set the clock**: Use Simulator's status bar override or a real device to show a clean time (e.g. 9:41 AM — Apple's canonical time)
- **Populate realistic data**: Log 3 of 5 prayers, have a Quran bookmark mid-surah, show a 15+ day streak. Empty states look lifeless.
- **Use London or a recognisable city**: Location text like "London, UK" or "New York, US" is relatable to a global audience.
- **Gold accent colour**: Use the default gold theme — it photographs well and matches the brand identity.
- **No personal data**: Double-check that no real names, emails, or identifiable info appear in screenshots.
- **Device frames**: Use Apple's official device frames from the Marketing Resources page, or tools like Rotato / Screenshots Pro. Match the device to the submission size (iPhone 16 Pro Max frame for 6.9" screenshots).
- **Consistency**: Same background style, same headline font/weight, same device frame across all 10. This creates a professional, cohesive strip in the App Store.

## App Preview Video

Optional but strongly recommended — apps with previews get significantly more taps.

**Specs**:
- Duration: 15-30 seconds (aim for ~25 seconds)
- Resolution: match the screenshot device size (1320 x 2868 for 6.9")
- Format: H.264, .mov or .mp4
- No letterboxing — full device frame recording
- Audio: optional ambient background (no voiceover required, but gentle nasheed-style music works well)

### Recommended Video Storyboard

| Time | Scene | What's Shown | Text Overlay |
|------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| 0-3s | Opening | App icon animates in, or cold launch splash ("Safa") | — |
| 3-7s | Home screen | Camera settles on home. Next prayer card with live countdown ticking. User scrolls slightly to show daily verse. | "Your Islamic companion" |
| 7-10s | Prayer logging | User taps a prayer dot — it fills in with a subtle animation + haptic feel. Toast shows "+10 Hasanat". | "Track your prayers" |
| 10-14s | Quran | Transition to Quran reader. Scroll through a few ayahs showing Arabic + translation. Zoom transition from surah list to reader if possible. | "Read with clarity" |
| 14-17s | Qibla | Compass view with the needle smoothly pointing to Makkah. Bearing number visible. | "Find Qibla anywhere" |
| 17-20s | Dhikr | Tasbeeh counter incrementing with each tap (show 3-4 taps). Count ring animates. | "Daily dhikr" |
| 20-23s | Live Activity | Cut to Lock Screen showing the Live Activity countdown. Then show Dynamic Island compact view. | "Always visible" |
| 23-25s | Dark mode | Quick montage: home screen, Quran reader, and prayer times in dark mode (0.7s each). | "Beautiful, day and night" |
| 25-28s | Closing | App icon centred with tagline. | "Safa — a calmer path to daily worship" |

### Video Capture Tips

- **Record on a real device** for smooth 120fps scrolling and natural gestures. Simulator recordings look robotic.
- **Use Screen Recording** (Settings > Control Centre > Screen Recording) or QuickTime via USB for higher quality.
- **Slow, deliberate gestures**: Swipe and tap at about 70% of your normal speed. Fast fingers look frantic on video.
- **Pre-populate data**: Same as screenshots — logged prayers, bookmarks, streaks. The app should look lived-in, not fresh-installed.
- **Avoid showing the status bar** if it contains personal info (carrier name, low battery). Use Airplane mode + full battery.
- **Edit in Final Cut Pro, iMovie, or ScreenFlow**: Add text overlays, smooth transitions (dissolve, not flashy), and trim dead frames between interactions.
- **No hands/fingers visible**: Use AssistiveTouch's on-screen indicator or edit it out. Apple's guidelines prohibit showing physical hands.
- **Submit one video per device size**: You can reuse the same recording scaled, but ideally record natively on the largest device.
