## Key Principles (Retained)

- Use haptics intentionally, not constantly
- Encode meaning in patterns (not just "buzz on tap")
- Retention through meaningful practice, not notification pressure
- Premium means everyone can use the app comfortably
- "Experience-complete" over "feature-complete"
